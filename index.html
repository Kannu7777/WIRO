WHO WE ARE

Decades of experience, built into every solution.

WIRO.IN is the digital presence of Bhai Papers Private Limited, a New Delhi-based company established in 1988.

With more than three decades of experience, the company operates across the paper, wiro and packaging industries.

Our product range includes Wiro, nylon coated wires, SBS boards/paper boards, printing papers, binding machinery, corner tapes and other solutions for professional and commercial applications.
