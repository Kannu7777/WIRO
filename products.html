Corner Tape for Rigid Boxes

Corner tape for rigid boxes is a specialized heat-activated adhesive tape used to seal the edges of rigid board boxes. Made with clear plastic film, it offers high tensile strength and is tear and moisture resistant. It offers excellent corner reinforcement with a clean finish and minimal visibility outside the box.

Applications include:

  • Luxury Gift Boxes
  • Electronics and Mobile Phone Boxes
  • Apparel and Footwear Boxes
  • Cosmetic and Perfume Packaging
  • Confectionery and Liquor Boxes
